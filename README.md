# Quraq

**Play:** https://knyshevas.github.io/quraq/

A wordless, meditative puzzle game about crossing a Kazakh patchwork quilt (*quraq*). A small needle-girl walks across the pieces of a quilt. Where one fabric meets another she stops, and you sew her a way across with one of three traditional ornaments: *koshkar muiyz* (ram's horns), *ala zhip* (the striped band) and *zhuldyz* (the star). Any path reaches the goal; the ornaments you choose stay in the quilt. Every round the quilt is laid out and dyed afresh.

The fabrics and ornaments come from two hand-made pillowcases by **@by.rabat**.

Tap to walk. When she stops at a seam, pick an ornament and sew. Works on phones and desktops.

This repository only hosts the playable build (branch `gh-pages`); the source is private.
